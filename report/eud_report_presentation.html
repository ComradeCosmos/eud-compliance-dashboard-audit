18 · Findings
Key Findings & Risks
Critical observations from the comprehensive platform analysis
⚠ CRITICAL — SLA Violation
Fleet compliance at 82.83% is 2.17 points below the 85% SLA mandate. 2,880 devices are currently non-compliant and require immediate remediation prioritization.
⚡ HIGH — Device Lifecycle Risk
1,336 devices in BROKEN/IN_REPAIR/DECOMMISSIONED states auto-score to 0.00. Without formal lifecycle decommission, they distort compliance metrics.
⚡ HIGH — EDR Coverage Gap
10% of active (IN_USE) devices have at least one missing SentinelOne or control agent, directly triggering the zero-tolerance score collapse rule.
ℹ INFO — BROKEN Score Bug
The device condition generator has a logic issue: the BROKEN branch (i%50===0) is unreachable since multiples of 50 are always caught by i%25===0 (IN_REPAIR) first.
✓ STRENGTH — Trend Improving
6-month trend shows consistent upward trajectory from 83.0% (Dec 2025) to 87.5% (May 2026). +2.4% QoQ demonstrates effective remediation programs.
✓ STRENGTH — AI Forecasting
LSTM drift predictor provides 7/14/30-day early warning capability, enabling proactive remediation before SLA violations materialize.
✓ STRENGTH — Dynamic Extensibility
Fuzzy column detection auto-registers new compliance telemetries without code changes, enabling rapid policy expansion from any data team's spreadsheet output.
↗ OPPORTUNITY — Semantic Reconciler
MiniLM-based device deduplication can reduce ghost/duplicate records currently inflating the non-compliant count and distorting fleet accuracy metrics.

Platform Description

The Thrawn EUD Compliance Dashboard is a premium, enterprise-grade End-User Device (EUD) Compliance & Risk Management Platform. It aggregates security logs, evaluates endpoint controls against strict operational frameworks (EDR, Zero Trust, Patching), calculates normalized device risk scores using an advanced penalty-based risk engine, and applies deep learning models to predict compliance score drift and resolve device duplicates. The platform is built as a modular, container-ready multi-layered microservices system spanning a high-fidelity SOC-grade HTML/JavaScript frontend, a Go-based compliance engine, Python-based PyTorch AI services, and PostgreSQL/TimescaleDB persistence.
